# Privacy Policy — DRAFT v2026-09 (not reviewed by counsel)

## Who is responsible
The **Broker** named in the footer is the data controller for your personal
data. **TradeXLabel** operates the platform on the Broker's behalf as a
processor under a data processing agreement. [[LAWYER: controller/processor
split, and whether TradeXLabel is an independent controller for platform
security telemetry.]]

## What we collect
- Identity and contact: name, email, phone, country, IP address at
  registration and login.
- Identity verification: documents and results returned by the KYC provider
  [[LAWYER: name the provider and its role]].
- Account and trading: balances, orders, positions, transactions, withdrawal
  requests including the destination you enter.
- CRM: notes, call logs and tasks created by the Broker's staff about you,
  marketing source (affiliate, campaign, UTM tags), approximate location
  derived from your IP.
- Technical: browser and device information, security logs, audit trail of
  actions on your account.

## Why (legal bases)
Contract performance (running your account), legal obligations
(AML/KYC, transaction reporting to regulators, record keeping), legitimate
interests (fraud prevention, security, service improvement), consent
(marketing communications; withdrawable at any time).

## Who receives it
The Broker's staff with a role that needs it; TradeXLabel as processor;
identity-verification, email delivery and price-data providers; payment
providers; regulators and auditors when the law requires. [[LAWYER: list of
sub-processors and international transfer mechanism.]]

## How long
See the retention schedule: trading and transaction records for the period
required by the Broker's regulator (typically 5 to 7 years), CRM data for
leads that never opened an account for [[LAWYER: 12 months?]], security logs
for 12 months.

## Your rights
Access, rectification, erasure (subject to record-keeping duties), restriction,
portability, objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your
supervisory authority. Requests go to the Broker's support address; identity
is verified before data is released.

## Security
Encryption in transit, encryption of sensitive stored secrets, two-factor
authentication for staff, append-only audit logs, segregated client-funds
ledger. No system is perfectly secure; we notify affected users and the
authority when the law requires.
